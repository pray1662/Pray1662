# Sources and verification notes

## Liturgical text

The fixed Morning and Evening Prayer material in V0.3 follows the Order for Morning Prayer and Order for Evening Prayer in the 1662 Book of Common Prayer. The Church of England publishes these services online and identifies its displayed text as reproduced by permission of the Crown's Patentee, Cambridge University Press.

Reference pages:
- https://www.churchofengland.org/prayer-and-worship/worship-texts-and-resources/book-common-prayer/order-morning-prayer
- https://www.churchofengland.org/prayer-and-worship/worship-texts-and-resources/book-common-prayer/order-evening-prayer
- https://www.churchofengland.org/prayer-and-worship/worship-texts-and-resources/book-common-prayer

The current Royal prayers use King Charles III and the Royal Family wording reflected in the Church of England's current BCP presentation.

**Rights note:** the Book of Common Prayer has an unusual Crown-rights position in the United Kingdom. V0.3 is a development prototype. Before treating substantial BCP text as cleared for unrestricted public republication, obtain/confirm the relevant permission from Cambridge University Press as Crown Patentee.

## Scripture

The app intentionally contains Scripture references only. It does not reproduce Bible text. It is designed to be used alongside a physical Bible.

## Lectionary and Psalter

The ordinary lesson table, Sunday/Holy-Day overrides, proper Psalms and 30-day Psalter logic are retained from V0.2.x and remain covered by the project test suite.
