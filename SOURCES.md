# Sources used for V0.2

## Primary working transcription

InterVarsity Press, *Book of Common Prayer: International Edition* — free “1662 Daily Office Lectionary” resource. The PDF reproduces:

- Lessons Proper for Sundays
- Lessons Proper for Holy Days — Mattins and Evensong
- Proper Psalms on Certain Days
- The January–December Calendar tables for Mattins and Evensong

## Cross-check

Lynda Howell / Society of Archbishop Justus, “The Kalendar, with the Table of Lessons (1662)”, including separate monthly tables and the original Proper Lessons tables.

## Authoritative BCP context

The Church of England’s Book of Common Prayer (1662) resource identifies the Calendar with the Table of Lessons, Proper Lessons, and related tables as BCP resources and notes the Crown/Cambridge reproduction position for the Prayer Book text.

## Data policy

Only bibliographic labels and Scripture references are stored in the app. No Bible translation text is included. Full Prayer Book prose is also outside V0.2.
